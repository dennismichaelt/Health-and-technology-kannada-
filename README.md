# Health-and-technology-kannada-
A Kannada academic term paper exploring the role of modern technology in healthcare, including Artificial Intelligence, Telemedicine, Blockchain, Genetic Engineering, Medical Robotics, Nanotechnology, and Personalized Medicine.
# ಆರೋಗ್ಯ ಮತ್ತು ತಂತ್ರಜ್ಞಾನ | Health and Technology

## Project Overview

This repository contains an academic term paper titled
"ಆರೋಗ್ಯ ಮತ್ತು ತಂತ್ರಜ್ಞಾನ" (Health and Technology).

The project explores the role of modern technological
developments in the healthcare industry and explains how
technology is improving medical diagnosis, treatment,
patient care, and healthcare management.

## Objectives

- Understand the role of Artificial Intelligence in healthcare.
- Explore telemedicine and remote healthcare services.
- Study Blockchain technology in medical data security.
- Understand genetic engineering and biotechnology.
- Explore medical robotics and automated surgery.
- Study nanotechnology and drug delivery systems.
- Understand modern medical imaging technologies.
- Explore biomedical data analytics and personalized medicine.

## Topics Covered

1. Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare
2. Telemedicine Services
3. Blockchain Technology in Healthcare
4. Biotechnology and Genetic Technology
5. Medical Robotics and Automated Surgery
6. Medical Nanotechnology and Drug Delivery Systems
7. Advanced Medical Imaging (MRI, CT, PET)
8. Biomedical Data Analytics
9. Personalized Medicine
